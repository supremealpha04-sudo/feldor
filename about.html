OUR MISSION

Detect Earlier.
Save More Lives.

At FELDOR_HEALTH, we believe that every person deserves access to world-class cancer detection. Our mission is to harness the power of artificial intelligence to make early cancer detection accessible, affordable, and accurate for everyone — regardless of geography, income, or background.

OUR VISION

A World Where No Cancer Goes Undetected

We envision a future where AI-powered screening is as routine as a blood pressure check. Where a single scan can detect multiple cancer types with near-perfect accuracy. Where geographic barriers to specialist care are eliminated through telemedicine.

By 2030, we aim to screen 100 million people globally, reducing late-stage cancer diagnoses by 80% and saving millions of lives through early intervention.

OUR STORY

From a Garage to Global Impact

FELDOR_HEALTH was founded in 2022 by a team of oncologists, AI researchers, and healthcare technologists who shared a common frustration: too many cancers were being detected too late. What started as a research project at Stanford University's medical AI lab has grown into a global platform serving 128+ hospitals and impacting over 12,000 lives.

Our breakthrough came when our AI model achieved 97.4% accuracy on lung cancer detection — outperforming even experienced radiologists on challenging cases. Today, we continue to push the boundaries of what's possible at the intersection of artificial intelligence and oncology.
