About Feldor Technology Ltd

We are a HealthTech company revolutionizing early cancer detection through advanced artificial intelligence. Our mission is to make world-class diagnostic capabilities accessible to every patient, everywhere.

Product Overview

How our AI-powered cancer detection platform works

AI-Driven Diagnostic Workflow

Our platform integrates seamlessly into existing hospital workflows, transforming how cancer is detected and diagnosed.

  • Patient medical data and diagnostic images are securely collected
  • Our AI model processes the data using trained algorithms
  • The system generates risk scores and clinical insights
  • Doctors review results and take timely medical action

Business Model

A scalable HealthTech revenue model designed for sustainable growth

Subscription (SaaS)

Recurring subscription fees from hospitals and clinics for platform access, including AI analysis, dashboard tools, and reporting features.

Licensing Agreements

Enterprise licensing with governments and large health systems for nationwide or regional deployment of our AI diagnostic technology.

Pay-Per-Scan

Flexible pay-per-scan or pay-per-patient pricing for smaller clinics and diagnostic centers with variable patient volumes.

Strategic Partnerships

Revenue-sharing partnerships with diagnostic and imaging centers, integrating our AI into their existing service offerings.

App Downloads

Monetization through major app store platforms worldwide, offering tiered access to premium features for individual users.

User Engagement

Revenue generated from time spent on platform and in-app activity, including premium insights and advanced analytics.

Target Users & Value Proposition

Designed to serve every stakeholder in the healthcare ecosystem

Patients

Earlier diagnosis leads to better outcomes. Reduced treatment costs through early intervention. Improved survival and quality of life with timely medical action.

Hospitals & Clinics

Faster and more accurate screening reduces backlogs. Reduced workload on specialists through AI assistance. Improved operational efficiency and patient throughput.

Doctors

AI-assisted clinical decision support enhances accuracy. Reduced diagnostic errors through pattern recognition. Confidence in early-stage detection with data-backed insights.

Government & Public Health

Population-wide cancer screening capabilities. Data-driven public health planning with real-time analytics. Reduced national healthcare burden through prevention.

Competitive Landscape

What sets Feldor Technology apart from existing alternatives

Existing Alternatives

  • Manual diagnosis by specialists — limited by human availability and prone to oversight
  • Traditional screening tools — expensive, time-consuming, and resource-intensive
  • Early-stage AI healthcare solutions — limited accuracy and narrow scope

Our Competitive Edge

  • Earlier detection capability — catching cancer at Stage I-II when treatment success is highest
  • Cost efficiency — reducing diagnostic costs by up to 70% compared to traditional methods
  • Scalable and adaptable AI model — trained on diverse populations and cancer types
  • Designed for both advanced and low-resource settings — works offline and with minimal infrastructure

Traction & Validation
