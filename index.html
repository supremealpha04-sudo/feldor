The Problem We're Solving

Cancer remains one of the leading causes of death worldwide. Here's why early detection matters.

Late Detection

70% of cancer cases are detected at advanced stages, reducing survival rates by up to 60% and limiting treatment options.

High Treatment Costs

Late-stage cancer treatment costs average $150,000+ per patient, compared to $30,000 for early-stage intervention.

Specialist Shortages

There is a global shortage of 4.3 million healthcare workers, with oncologists being among the most critically needed.

Accessibility Challenges

Over 50% of the world's population lacks access to timely cancer screening, especially in rural and underserved regions.
