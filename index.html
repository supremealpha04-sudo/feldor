The Challenge

The Problem

Cancer remains one of the leading causes of death globally, largely because it is often detected at advanced stages when treatment options are limited and expensive.

🔍

Subtle Early Symptoms

Early-stage cancer symptoms are subtle and frequently missed during routine examinations, leading to delayed diagnosis and poorer outcomes.

🏥

Limited Specialist Access

Access to oncologists and diagnostic specialists is limited, especially in developing regions where healthcare infrastructure is scarce.

⏱️

Costly & Slow Screening

Traditional screening methods are costly, slow, and heavily dependent on human expertise, creating bottlenecks in patient care.

💰

Enormous Healthcare Costs

Late diagnosis leads to higher mortality, emotional trauma, and enormous healthcare costs that strain individuals and systems alike.

Investor Insight: Late detection increases treatment costs by multiples and places unsustainable strain on healthcare systems worldwide.
