The Challenge

Understanding Early Cancer Detection Barriers

🔍

Subtle Early Symptoms

Early-stage cancer symptoms are often overlooked during routine examinations. These subtle signs are easily missed by both patients and healthcare providers, leading to delayed diagnosis and significantly poorer treatment outcomes when intervention finally occurs.

📊 Impact: 40% of cancers diagnosed at advanced stages
🏥

Limited Specialist Access

Access to specialized oncologists and diagnostic experts is severely limited, particularly in developing regions. This creates critical healthcare inequality, leaving millions without access to the expertise needed for accurate early detection and proper diagnosis.

📊 Impact: 75% of population lacks specialist access
⏱️

Costly & Slow Screening

Traditional screening methods are expensive, time-consuming, and heavily dependent on human expertise. The lengthy diagnostic process creates bottlenecks in patient care, straining healthcare system resources and delaying critical treatment decisions.

📊 Impact: 2-4 weeks average diagnostic time
💰

Enormous Healthcare Costs

Late diagnosis dramatically increases treatment costs, mortality rates, and emotional burden on families. The financial and social strain affects individuals, healthcare systems, and entire economies, creating a cycle of preventable suffering and economic burden.

💵 Impact: $200K+ average cost per patient
