The Problem

Challenges in Early Cancer Detection

Subtle Early Symptoms

Early-stage cancer symptoms are often overlooked during routine examinations. These subtle signs are easily missed by both patients and healthcare providers, leading to delayed diagnosis when treatment options are more limited.

Impact: 40% diagnosed at advanced stages

Limited Specialist Access

Access to specialized oncologists and diagnostic experts is severely limited, particularly in developing regions. This creates a critical gap in healthcare accessibility and leads to preventable deaths.

Impact: 75% lack specialist access

Costly & Slow Screening

Traditional screening methods are expensive, time-consuming, and heavily dependent on human expertise. The lengthy diagnostic process creates bottlenecks in patient care and strains healthcare resources.

Impact: 2-4 weeks average diagnostic time

Enormous Healthcare Costs

Late diagnosis dramatically increases treatment costs, mortality rates, and emotional burden on families. The financial strain affects individuals, healthcare systems, and economies globally.

Impact: $200K+ average cost per patient
