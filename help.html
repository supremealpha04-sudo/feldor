Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI detector work?
Our AI detector uses advanced deep learning algorithms trained on millions of medical images. It analyzes uploaded scans for patterns and anomalies that may indicate cancer, providing results with confidence scores in under 5 minutes.
Is my medical data secure?
Yes, all medical data is encrypted using industry-standard protocols. We comply with healthcare data protection regulations and never share your information with third parties without explicit consent.
What image formats are supported?
We support JPG, PNG, and DICOM (medical imaging) formats. Our system automatically detects and processes the appropriate format for analysis.
How accurate is the AI detection?
Our system achieves 95%+ accuracy in clinical validation studies. However, it's designed as a decision support tool and should always be used in conjunction with professional medical evaluation.
How do I contact support?
You can reach our support team via email at support@feldorhealth.com, phone at +234 800 000 0000, or through our social media channels. We're available 24/7 to assist you.
